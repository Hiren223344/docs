## Join the Project

Want to contribute to Fearch? We are fully open-source and welcome pull requests regarding:
- Optimizing Markdown parsing speeds.
- Expanding Python, Node.js, and Go SDK capabilities.
- Resolving anti-bot rendering challenges on complex single-page apps.

Feel free to connect with our engineers or submit issue logs directly on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/frenix-sh).
